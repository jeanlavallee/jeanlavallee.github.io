Data resources

Paris Trade Directories, 1787–1914
with Pierre-Philippe Combes, Laurent Gobillon, Gabriel Loumeau, and Yanos Zylberberg

An address-level panel of Parisian businesses built from 293,000 pages of the city’s trade directories. Each business is geocoded on 19th-century maps and linked across years.

Website online in December 2026
National Policies Database
with Livia Haddad, Philippe Laban, Ben Marx, Vincent Pons, and Vincent Rollet

A worldwide dataset of national policies extracted with large language models from 155,913 country reports. It records policies in more than 200 countries since 1945, classified into 21 topics and placed on ideological scales.

Website online in December 2026
