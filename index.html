I work in spatial economics, public economics, and political economy. I study the spatial distribution of economic activity, how it changes over time as a function of public policy, and how these policies are shaped by political incentives. My current projects measure how transit changes where people live and work in French cities, and how the ideology of elected leaders shapes policymaking around the world. I studied economics at the École normale supérieure Paris-Saclay.
